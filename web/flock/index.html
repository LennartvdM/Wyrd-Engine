Each activity's own spread

One panel per activity, un-stacked. On the stacked chart a boundary carries the cumulative spread of everything beneath it, and the top boundary is pinned at 100 %, so its bands are not each activity's own variation. Here each panel is one activity's own spread, demarcated by the settings above; the caption under the figure says exactly what is drawn.

The same shares, without edges

The stack above draws a boundary between every pair of activities. These do not: on the left, every column is the whole population mixed in proportion; on the right, one row per activity where stronger colour means more people.

Every day, stacked

One row per person-day, midnight to midnight. The totals above are an average and cannot show how a transition is spread; here the days themselves lie on top of each other, so a population that moves together reads as a straight edge and one that does not reads as a frayed one.

Binned shares as a table
